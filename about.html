About Forge Events

One place to discover events, register to attend, and make event-day access simpler.

Discover

Find upcoming campus and community events, with the essential date, venue and event information in one place.

Register

Give attendees a straightforward path from discovering an event to signing up and preparing to attend.

Manage

Give organisers a starting point to create events and keep event information organised.

Our goal

Forge Events is designed to make event coordination easier for organisers and clearer for attendees. The wider platform can support registration, digital tickets, check-in and attendance reporting as those services are connected.
